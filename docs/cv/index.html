02

Research experience

Laboratories, IESP-UERJ

  1. 01/2026 – present

    NECON (IESP-UERJ)

    Researcher at the Center for Studies on Congress, coordinated by Fabiano Santos. I work on the lab's databases on roll-call votes and legislative production.

    Selected delivery A technical note applying spatial models of voting to the 2026 presidential race.

  2. 05/2025 – present

    DOXA (IESP-UERJ)

    Researcher at the Laboratory of Electoral Studies, Political Communication and Public Opinion, coordinated by Fernando Meireles and Argelina Cheibub Figueiredo. I work on the design and analysis of the lab's public opinion surveys, including weighting and post-stratification.

    Selected delivery Analysis of the DOXA survey on how the population of the state of Rio de Janeiro learns about politics, now a DOXA discussion paper.

  3. 04/2025 – present

    MAPE (IESP-UERJ)

    Researcher at the Laboratory for Monitoring and Evaluation of Policies and Elections, coordinated by Fernando Meireles and Bruno Schaefer. I build and maintain the mape_municipios database and its public interface, and teach in the lab's methodological training.

    Selected delivery A Streamlit application that lets users interact with mape_municipios, a database covering 30 years and 451 variables on Brazilian municipalities.

Funded projects

  1. 07/2026 – present

    Pensando o Direito research grant, MJSP

    Researcher. Systematic review of the evidence on a set of public security policies for the Legal Amazon, organized around the idea of a qualified entry of the State into vulnerable territories, following the OQF (O Que Funciona?) protocol developed at MAPE. I run the search protocol, screening, and evidence extraction. Project coordinated by Djane Braz Duarte. Expected deliveries: (i) an analytical report for the Ministry; (ii) an article submitted to a peer-reviewed journal; (iii) entries on the reviewed policies in the Dicionário de Favelas Marielle Franco.

  2. 08/2025 – present

    RESA project, UFAC and IESP-UERJ

    Researcher on the socioeconomic and environmental impacts of family-based agroextractive production in the Chico Mendes Extractive Reserve, in Acre, with an emphasis on what cooperatives change in land use and in household income. I handle the quantitative data and build the project's public monitoring platform. Coordinated by Luci Maria Teston (UFAC), with IESP-UERJ as a partner institution.

    Selected delivery A preliminary version of the monitoring platform, covering deforestation in Acre.

Earlier positions

  1. 02/2025 – 04/2025

    School of Public and Business Administration, FGV EBAPE

    Research Assistant. Digitized and systematized historical election results using OCR and LLMs, under the supervision of Prof. Cesar Zucco, Ph.D.

    Selected delivery Databases extracted from historical images, source code, and a technical report.

  2. 10/2024 – 02/2025

    CEBRAP

    Consultant for the Center for Critical Imagination. Applied OCR and LLMs to the Brazilian Statistical Yearbooks, published as manuscripts, to systematize social assistance data since the early 20th century. In collaboration with José Szwako, Ph.D.

    Selected delivery Databases extracted from PDFs, source code, and a technical report.

  3. 06/2019 – 09/2022

    School of Social Sciences, FGV CPDOC

    Junior Research Fellow. Supported by an FGV/CNPq fellowship, examined Executive-Legislative relations in the early 20th century using qualitative and quantitative methods. Supervised by Prof. Jaqueline Porto Zulini, Ph.D.

    Selected delivery Three technical reports and one peer-reviewed article.
