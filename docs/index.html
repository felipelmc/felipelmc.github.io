Felipe Lamarca

M.A. student in Political Science, IESP-UERJ
Data scientist, AtlasIntel

I am a data scientist and a social scientist, trained at FGV EMAp and FGV CPDOC. I use statistical and computational methods to understand political and social phenomena, such as elections, legislative behavior, public opinion and public policy.

I do research at MAPE, DOXA and NECON.
